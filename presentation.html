How to Recognize Phishing

Email

Check the sender

Does the email address look official? Check the complete domain.

support@paypa1.com
support@paypal.com
Lien

Hover over links

Before clicking, check where the link really leads.

http://bit.ly/3xYz123
https://www.paypal.com/login
Orthographe

Spelling and grammar

Phishing emails often contain obvious spelling and grammar mistakes.

Your account has been deactivated
Urgence

Urgency and threats

Be wary of messages designed to create a sense of urgency.

Action immediate requise !
Piece jointe

Suspicious attachments

Do not open unsolicited attachments.

facture_2026.exe
facture_2026.pdf
HTTPS

Check HTTPS

Secure connections use HTTPS, but HTTPS alone does not prove legitimacy.

http://paypal-secure.com
https://www.paypal.com

Real-world Examples

Phishing Online Bank

Subject: ALERT - Account locked!

Dear customer,

We detected suspicious activity on your account.

Click here immediately to confirm your identity

or your account will be permanently suspended.

http://banque-securite-urgent.com

Red flags:

  • Excessive urgency
  • Suspicious link outside the official domain
  • Request for sensitive information
Legitimate Online Bank

Subject: Account statement - March 2026

Hello [First name],

Your monthly account statement is available.

Sign in to your customer area to view it.

https://www.ma-banque.fr/compte

Trust signals:

  • No excessive urgency
  • Valid HTTPS link
  • No request for sensitive information
Phishing Google

Subject: Your password is about to expire

Bonjour,

Your Gmail password expires in 24 hours.

Update it now to avoid losing access.

http://google-account-update.net

Red flags:

  • Google does not send this type of email
  • Domain differs from google.com
  • Artificial sense of urgency

Best Practices

01

Always verify

Before clicking, verify the sender, link, and context.

02

Two-factor authentication

Enable 2FA on all important accounts.

03

Regular updates

Keep your systems and software up to date.

04

Continuous learning

Stay informed about new phishing techniques.

05

Report attacks

Report suspicious emails to your IT team.

06

Unique passwords

Use a different password for every account.
